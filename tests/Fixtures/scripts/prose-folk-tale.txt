Long ago, in a village beside a wide river, there lived a girl named Ada.
Ada was known for her stubbornness and for the red cloth she always wore.

Every morning Ada walked to the market to sell her mother's cassava.
The traders knew her by name and saved her the shaded stall.

One evening a storm came. The river rose in the darkness and the village
feared for its boats. Ada ran to the bank alone.

By morning the water had fallen. The village found Ada asleep in the grass,
and every boat still tied where it had been. They carried her home singing.
